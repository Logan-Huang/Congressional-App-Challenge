How this works

  1. Find your water system. We match your address or zip code to the utility that most likely serves it.
  2. Read the EPA record. We look at violations from the federal drinking-water database.
  3. Explain it simply. A green, yellow or red result, a few plain sentences, and what you can do next.

Not a substitute for your utility's annual water quality report (the Consumer Confidence Report). You can ask your water provider for a copy. This site never says water is "safe" or "unsafe"; it reports what public records show.

How matching works: a street address is placed on a map and checked against EPA's service-area boundaries. A zip code is matched by its center point and by the places each utility lists as served, so zip matches are approximate.
