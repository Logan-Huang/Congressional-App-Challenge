Is my tap water meeting federal standards?

Enter your zip code. We check U.S. EPA records and explain what we find in plain English.

Data comes from the U.S. EPA Safe Drinking Water Information System (SDWIS). Federal data is updated quarterly, so very recent changes may not appear yet.

Matching a zip code to a water system is approximate. Zip codes and water service areas do not line up exactly.

This is not a substitute for your utility's annual water quality report (Consumer Confidence Report), which you can request from your water provider.
